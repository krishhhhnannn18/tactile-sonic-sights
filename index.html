SENSE · INTERPRET · ALERT
The challenge

Some obstacles are hard to anticipate.

White canes are valuable mobility aids, but they may not detect every obstacle above waist level. Low branches, projecting signboards, open vehicle doors, and wall-mounted objects can create unexpected hazards in busy public spaces.
Our proposed approach

Environmental awareness, delivered as a tactile cue.

Tactile Sonic Sights combines sensing technologies with vibration-based feedback, aiming to provide useful alerts without adding spoken instructions that could compete with surrounding sounds.

⌁

Haptic alerts

Vibration cues are intended to communicate detected hazards quietly and intuitively.
